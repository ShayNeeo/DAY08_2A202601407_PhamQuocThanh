VINUNIVERSITY’S Ph.D. IN COMPUTER SCIENCE SCHOLARSHIP – NOW OPEN FOR 2025–2026 ADMISSIONS!

The admission cycle for VinUniversity’s Ph.D. in Computer Science program for the 2025 – 2026 academic year officially kicks off on March 1st, 2025! With generous 100% scholarships and an ideal research environment, VinUniversity is seeking talented Ph.D. candidates to join us in putting Vietnamese academia on the global map and driving scientific innovations for the benefit of the community.

Full sponsorship of PhD program tuition and monthly allowance up to 20,000,000 VND/month.

Study duration: 3 – 4 years, full-time.

Exclusive Benefits for Doctoral Students at VinUniversity

100% of students have the opportunity to apply for Vingroup’s 1,100 scholarship program to participate in research for 1 – 2 years at a VinUniversity’s partner university (scholarship includes all fees and tuition), living expenses, health insurance, round-trip airfare and related expenses).

Students are directly guided by leading professors currently teaching at VinUniversity. All lecturers actively participate in research in their fields of expertise and are in the top 2% of most cited professors across all scientific fields. In addition, you will also be collaboratively supervised by world-famous professors from VinUniversity’s partner universities internationally.

Students have access to state-of-the-art facilities, including super labs, 24/7 project labs, and rapid processing rooms. They can also utilize VinUniversity’s library, which provides access to leading scientific databases, including IEEE and ACM for computer science research.

Students will be supported with necessary and appropriate equipment to use for work.

Conditions and Application Documents

Admission conditions:

Applicants for the PhD in Computer Science program must meet the following conditions:

Have graduated with a Master’s degree or a Bachelor’s degree with honors or higher in Computer Science or related fields;

Have research experience demonstrated through a master’s thesis of a research-oriented training program; or published scientific articles or reports; or have worked for 2 years (24 months) or more as a lecturer or researcher at training establishments and science and technology organizations;

Have a draft research outline and expected study and research plan for the entire course.

English requirements for applicants:

Possessing an English certificate of IELTS 6.5 or higher (or equivalent) within 2 years (24 months) before the date of enrollment or graduation from master’s and/or university degrees in programs taught in English.

For candidates who do not satisfy the above conditions, candidates need to:

At the time of admission: candidates need to have an IELTS of 5.5 or higher or equivalent as prescribed in Appendix II of Circular No. 18/2021/TT-BGDDT.

Within 12 months from the admission date: candidates need to supplement a valid IELTS 6.5 or higher certificate (or equivalent).

Meet the conditions of the research outline and research experience.

Admission application documents

Statement of Purpose (maximum 2 pages) answers the questions: (1) Why did the candidate choose to study for a PhD?; (2) Reasons for choosing VinUni; (3) Share the candidate’s research experience and passion; (4) Future plans after graduating with a PhD.

CV summarizing personal information in English.

Official transcript of most recent level of study – Undergraduate or Postgraduate.

English certificate IELTS (IE Academic) 6.5 or higher, or other equivalent certificates valid for 02 years.

02 letters of recommendation from Professors/Lecturers who have guided the candidate.

Link to the source of published scientific articles or scans of published articles (if any).

Admission Process Information

10 graduate students for the 2025 – 2026 school year.

Applicants are required to submit applications according to VinUniversity’s instructions on the official admission portal

. Qualified candidates will be invited to a Technical Interview round with VinUni Professors. Admission decisions will be made by the Admissions Committee and the Program Director will notify the candidates of the results.

Application deadline for Round 1: until April 30th, 2025.

Application deadline for Round 2: TBA depending on the admission results from Round 1.

Interview & receive results: 01 month after submitting application.

Estimated on-board time: On a rolling basis.
